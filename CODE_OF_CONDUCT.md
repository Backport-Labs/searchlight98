# Code of Conduct

## Expected behaviour

Participants in this project are expected to:

- Be respectful of differing experience, opinions and backgrounds.
- Keep discussion on the subject of the project.
- Give and accept criticism of work in a constructive way.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming otherwise.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information about another person without permission.
- Conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to the issues, pull requests and discussions of this repository.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that do not follow
this code, and may block a participant from the repository.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.
